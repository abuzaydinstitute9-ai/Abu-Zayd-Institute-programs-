Who we are

Our mission and our vision

Abu Zayd Institute is the teaching arm of Abu Zayd Solutions — a growing family of Islamic initiatives built one honest step at a time.

Mission

To teach the Qur'an, tajweed, hadith, and Islamic Studies to children and adults in a way that's affordable, structured, and rooted in sincerity — and to guide youth and campus Muslims through the real pressures of their environment.

Vision

To grow Abu Zayd Solutions into a full ecosystem — Institute, Publications, a daily da'wah presence, a charity arm, and physical schools from primary through university level, in shaa Allah.

The founder

Suleiman Adam Muhammad Gbale

Known as Abu Zayd — 300-level Islamic Studies student, Kwara State University, Malete
"I learn, build, and grow in the process."

Abu Zayd is an author, a teacher, an imperfect mentor, an observer, and above all — a student of knowledge. He founded Abu Zayd Institute not from a place of having arrived, but from a place of walking the path and inviting others to walk it with him. He is the author of Purpose Before Degree: A Muslim Guide to University Life, published under Abu Zayd Publications and available on Amazon.
